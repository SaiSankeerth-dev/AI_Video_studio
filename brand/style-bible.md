# ContentFactory Brand Style Bible: Fern-Style Investigative Documentary

## 1. Philosophical North Star
ContentFactory produces **investigative, evidence-first, cinematic documentaries** about technology, systems, and human agency. We reject the generic "AI slideshow" aesthetic (floating abstract holograms, neon grids, ungrounded futuristic robots). Every visual must answer:
> **"Why is the viewer seeing this right now, and what physical event or evidence does it prove?"**

---

## 2. Visual Identity & Grammar
Our aesthetic is **investigative + cinematic + technological + restrained**.

### A. Camera Language
- **Virtual Camera**: Every frame possesses subtle physical momentum (100% to 105% slow drift, gradual push-in, or horizontal dolly). Never perfectly static for >2 seconds.
- **Framing**: Wide contextual room -> Medium subject -> Macro screen/hand detail.
- **Depth of Field**: Shallow depth of field for human/hardware moments; crisp hyper-focus for official documents and data evidence.
- **Motion Match & Transitions**: Cuts are editorial, not decorative. Hard cuts for revelation and tension; match cuts for continuity of action; sound bridges to lead picture.

### B. Lighting & Environment
- **Atmosphere**: Practical low-key lighting. 2:13 AM workstation glow, dark concrete server corridors, tungsten desk lamps against cold cyan monitor displays.
- **Color Grading**: Cold neutral shadows (#0a0d12), charcoal backgrounds (#12161f), restrained warm amber warning accents (#e69d45), subdued clinical cyan highlights (#4facfe), paper evidence ivory (#f3f4f6).

### C. The 15 Documentary Visual Categories
1. `REAL_FOOTAGE`: Authentic archival recordings, public hearings, field events.
2. `ARCHIVAL_PHOTO`: Historical press releases, laboratory archives, dated photographs.
3. `OFFICIAL_DOCUMENT`: SEC filings, arXiv papers, OpenAI technical reports, patents.
4. `SCREEN_RECONSTRUCTION`: Pixel-accurate OS, browsers, terminal execution, IDEs.
5. `SCREEN_RECORDING`: Real software navigation, cursor dynamics, command execution.
6. `AI_REENACTMENT`: Photorealistic reconstructions of unwitnessed human/historical moments.
7. `BLENDER_3D`: Spatial architecture, neural attention geometry, server topology.
8. `REMOTION_GRAPHIC`: Editorial charts, comparisons, timeline cards, data counters.
9. `DATA_VISUALIZATION`: Multi-dimensional metrics, benchmark curves, error rates.
10. `EVIDENCE_CARD`: Zoomed document excerpts with highlighted text and verified source badges.
11. `KINETIC_TYPOGRAPHY`: Hard-hitting thesis statements, impactful question typography.
12. `ENVIRONMENT_SHOT`: Exterior tech campuses, midnight office towers, data facility hulls.
13. `HUMAN_MOMENT`: Hands lifting off keyboard, furrowed brow, physical reactions to autonomy.
14. `MACRO_OBJECT`: Mechanical keyboard switches, silicon dies, blinking server LEDs, mouse clicks.
15. `ABSTRACT_TRANSITION`: Dark screen blackout, interface static pulse, audio-led bridge.

## 3. Recurring Visual Motifs
To establish visual cohesion and psychological tension, we weave specific motifs across chapters:
- **The Cursor**: Autonomous, deliberate, hesitation before execution.
- **The Physical Clock**: Late night markers (02:13 AM), timestamps of silent execution.
- **The Hands**: Human touch relinquishing the keyboard; the threshold of manual vs agentic control.
- **The Terminal**: Monospace shell logs, return codes (0 vs non-zero), real-time stdout streams.
- **The Document**: Highlighted text lines, official stamps, date markers.

---

## 4. Spoken Narration Rules
- **Write for the Ear, Not the Page**: Short, punchy sentences, contractions, natural cadence.
- **Strictly Banned Jargon**: "Furthermore", "Moreover", "It is important to note", "In conclusion", "This demonstrates that".
- **Dynamic Cadence**:
  - Deliberate Revelation: 110–130 WPM.
  - Investigative Flow: 135–155 WPM.
  - Montage / Escalation: 165–180 WPM.
- **Strategic Pauses**: 250ms cadence pauses, 600ms mid-thought breaths, 1000ms revelation silences.

---

## 5. Sound Design Architecture
Every scene is built from 8 discrete audio layers:
1. `VOICE`: Natural, dry, lightly compressed, centered narration.
2. `ROOM_TONE`: Constant low-frequency room tone / HVAC air hum.
3. `SCORE`: Tension drones, minimalist analog synth pulses, investigative bass lines.
4. `FOLEY`: Physical mechanical keystrokes, mouse clicks, chair creaks, paper rustles.
5. `UI_SFX`: Subtle interface clicks, terminal bell chirps, modal notifications.
6. `SUB_BASS_HITS`: Low-end punctuation (30–60 Hz) on revelations and blackouts.
7. `SOUND_BRIDGES`: SFX preceding visual cut by 200–400ms.
8. `DELIBERATE_SILENCE`: Complete audio drops to emphasize critical questions.

---

## 6. Packaging & Thumbnail Philosophy
- **Thumbnail**: 1 dominant subject, 1 visual conflict, 1 curiosity gap. Maximum 2–5 bold words. Must be instantly legible at 320×180 thumbnail size.
- **Title**: Contextual anchor that balances the thumbnail's mystery with factual substance.
