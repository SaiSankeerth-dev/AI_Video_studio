# Agent Talk — Brand Profile

## 1. Brand Identity & Overview
- **Brand Name:** Agent Talk
- **Handles:** 
  - Instagram: `@agent.talk`
  - YouTube: `@AgentTalk` / Agent Talk Shorts
  - TikTok: `@agent.talk`
- **Tagline:** Intelligent AI Agent Stories for Developers & Builders.
- **Mission:** Demystify autonomous AI agents, coding agents, and modern developer workflows through entertaining, cinematic, relatable, and technically accurate storytelling.
- **Positioning:** Where real developer reality meets autonomous AI humor. We are not a generic AI news curator, nor do we peddle breathless AI hype or fake productivity hacks. We showcase what it actually feels like when autonomous agents enter our terminals, codebases, and daily lives.

---

## 2. Brand Personality & Archetype
- **Archetype:** The Clever Protagonist & Curious Hacker (Creator + Jester + Sage).
- **Tone Matrix:**
  - **Entertaining:** Witty dialogue, comedic developer suffering (merge conflicts, rogue commits, runaway loops).
  - **Intelligent:** Accurate engineering terminology (AST diffs, context windows, MCP servers, sandboxing, Docker containers).
  - **Cinematic:** High-production 3D aesthetic, moody neon/warm desk lighting, deliberate framing, rhythmic pacing.
  - **Relatable:** Captures late-night coding anxiety, the fear of approving terminal diffs, and the thrill of tools that actually work.
  - **Informative:** Always explains the real mechanism behind tools (open source, BYOS, self-hosting, local LLMs).

---

## 3. Recurring Characters

### Claude (Original Mascot)
- **Role:** The brilliant, literal-minded, hyper-fast autonomous AI coding agent. Perched on books or desk corners, perpetually ready to refactor 4,000 files without hesitation.
- **Visual Identity:**
  - Cute low-poly 3D orange/peach/terracotta box-shaped character.
  - Slightly rounded rectangular body.
  - Two upright black rectangular/square eyes with expressive tilt.
  - Two short block arms and four short block-shaped legs.
  - Warm illuminated glow accents.
- **Crucial Rule:** Claude is an original creative mascot representing autonomous agents. He is never redesigned, never made humanoid, and never confused with official corporate mascots or logos.

### Tariq Vance (Human Developer Co-Star)
- **Role:** The grounded, caffeine-powered senior developer navigating the chaotic AI revolution. The voice of reason, skepticism, and hard-earned production wisdom.
- **Visual Identity:** Gold wireframe glasses, ocean-teal knitted beanie, navy zip bomber jacket, expressive brows, desk setup.

---

## 4. Brand Standards & Rules

### What Agent Talk IS:
1. **Fact-First:** Every tool, library, benchmark, and workflow discussed must exist and be verifiable.
2. **Humor Rooted in Truth:** The jokes land because every software engineer has lived the horror of breaking production.
3. **Respectful of Audience Intelligence:** We talk to developers as peers, not novices needing "AI for Beginners" fluff.
4. **Platform-Native:** Formatting, hooks, and aspect ratios tailored specifically for 9:16 vertical short-form video.

### What Agent Talk NEVER Does:
1. **No Breathless AI Hype:** Never use buzzwords like "This changes EVERYTHING!", "RIP Software Engineers!", "10x your life in 2 seconds!".
2. **No Fake Statistics:** Never invent benchmarks, view counts, or user numbers.
3. **No Keyword Stuffing or Emoji Spam:** Keep captions clean, readable, and naturally indexed.
4. **No Automated or Robotic Tone:** Speak with human cadence, warmth, and sharp comedic timing.
5. **No Engagement Bait:** No "Comment 'AI' for a secret link", no fake giveaways, no manufactured controversy.
