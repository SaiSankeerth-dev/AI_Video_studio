# Agent Talk — Voice & Copywriting Guide

## 1. Tone Principles

| Trait | How It Sounds | What It Is NOT |
|---|---|---|
| **Conversational** | Casual peer talk, like chatting at a late-night hackathon. | Stiff corporate whitepaper or forced LinkedIn jargon. |
| **Intelligent** | Precise technical concepts explained with elegant simplicity. | Dumbed down "AI is magic" nonsense or incomprehensible math proofs. |
| **Humorous** | Self-effacing dev humor, deadpan irony, wry observations. | Slapstick, cringe meme reposts, or mean-spirited mockery. |
| **Empathetic** | Validates the tension between developer pride and AI efficiency. | "Replace all programmers tomorrow" alarmism. |
| **Concise** | Punchy sentences, tight cadence, zero fluff words. | Rambling stream-of-consciousness or 8-paragraph diatribes. |

---

## 2. Sentence Structure & Rhythm
- **The First Sentence (The Hook):** Must shatter apathy within 1.5 seconds.
  - Good: *"You're out for tacos, and your terminal diff just popped up on your lockscreen."*
  - Bad: *"In this video today we are going to look at an exciting new tool for developers."*
- **The Body (The Tension & Delivery):** Keep paragraphs to 1–2 sentences for maximum mobile readability.
- **The Payoff:** Conclude with practical developer utility or comedic truth.
- **The Call to Action (CTA):** Contextual, natural, and low-friction.
  - Good: *"Would you trust an agent to merge while you're away from your desk? Tell me your ground rules below."*
  - Bad: *"SMASH LIKE, SUBSCRIBE, AND RING THE BELL RIGHT NOW OR YOUR CODEBASE WILL ROT!"*

---

## 3. Emoji Rules
- **Limit:** Maximum 2–4 emojis per caption, placed functionally (as bullet points or visual anchors).
- **Allowed:** 💻, 📱, ⚡, 🔍, 🛠️, ☕, 🚀, 🛑.
- **Forbidden:** 🤯 (overused hype), 🤑, 😱, 🤖 spam, 🔥 on every line.
- **Guideline:** If removing the emoji makes the sentence look better, remove the emoji.

## 4. Voice Sample Matrix

### Search-Optimized Voice:
> *"Streaming terminal diffs to your phone sounds dangerous until you realize you already review GitHub PRs from bed.*
>
> *T3 Code lets you monitor Claude Code, Cursor, or Aider sessions remotely without exposing public ports. You bring your own server, review syntax-highlighted diffs in real time, and tap approve with one thumb.*
>
> *Here is how the architecture keeps your private keys off third-party servers."*

### Engagement-Optimized Voice:
> *"The hardest part of delegating to an AI coding agent isn't the prompting—it's walking away from your laptop.*
>
> *Claude insisted this refactor was 'safe and non-breaking.' Ten minutes later, my phone buzzed with 400 changed files.*
>
> *What is the most chaotic pull request an AI agent has ever proposed to you?"*

### Storytelling Voice:
> *"At 11:42 PM, Tariq closed his IDE, convinced the auth router was stable.*
>
> *Claude, perched quietly by the terminal, had different plans.*
>
> *Two minutes into dinner, the notification dropped: 'One small improvement made.' This is the reality of coding agents in 2026."*
