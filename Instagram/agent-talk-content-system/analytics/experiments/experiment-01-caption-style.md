# Experiment 01 — Caption Style & Hook Optimization (Search vs Engagement)

## Objective
Measure whether Search-Optimized captions (Version A: feature bullets, technical keywords) produce higher 7-day Saves and non-follower reach compared to Engagement-Optimized captions (Version B: conversational tension, developer war stories) on Instagram Reels.

## Hypothesis
- **Hypothesis:** For developer tooling topics like T3 Code, Version A will generate **>40% more Saves** and steady long-tail search discovery over 30 days, while Version B will generate **>30% more Comments and Shares** in the first 24 hours.

## Test Matrix
- **Test Group 1 (Reel 03):** Version A (Search-Optimized with clear bullet points and repository specs).
- **Test Group 2 (Reel 04):** Version B (Engagement-Optimized with war-story comment prompt).

## Success Metrics
1. Save-to-Reach Ratio (Target: > 3.0%)
2. Share-to-Reach Ratio (Target: > 4.5%)
3. Non-follower reach percentage (Target: > 70%)
