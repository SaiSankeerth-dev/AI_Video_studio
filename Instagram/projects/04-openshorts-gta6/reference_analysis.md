## 1. Storytelling & Narrative Structure

### Core Concept
Can you actually get rich by using AI to automatically cut GTA 6 live streams into viral short-form clips?

### 3-Part Structural Arc
1. **The Hook & Roadmap (0.0s – 13.8s):**
   - *Hook:* "Claude. GTA 6 comes out next week. People say you can make money from it. Is that true? Let me explain."
   - *Roadmap Anchor:* Lays out a clean 3-part mental model upfront ("Three simple parts: What people say, How to do it free, and Is it real?"). This keeps the viewer watching to reach part 3.
2. **Part 1 — What People Say (13.8s – 25.7s):**
   - The hype pitch: take long streams -> cut highlights -> post everywhere -> get paid for views.
   - Sarcastic reality check: "So I post other people's gameplay? That's the idea."
3. **Part 2 — How To Do It Free (25.7s – 37.4s):**
   - Introduces the real open-source tool: `mutonby/openshorts` (7.1k stars, MIT on GitHub).
   - Shows UI demo card: paste stream link -> AI detects highlights, reframes to 9:16, burns captions.
   - Character banter: "It edits too? What do I do?" -> "Press post. Your specialty."
4. **Part 3 — Is It Real? / The Reality Check (37.4s – 48.1s):**
   - The fact check: Money is real, BUT footage belongs to Rockstar, YouTube is tightening reused-content rules, and millions of accounts will spam the same clips on Day 1.
   - Gauge meter visual: Shows needle swinging between LATE and EARLY.
   - Comedic punchline: "So am I rich?" -> "You're early. Not rich."
