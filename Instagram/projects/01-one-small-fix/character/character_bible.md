# Character Bible — *One Small Fix*

Project: `01-one-small-fix`  
Visual World: Stylized faceted low-poly / matte-vinyl 3D miniature studio (`1080 × 1920`, `9:16`)

---

## Primary Human Character — Tariq Vance

- **Role:** Indie SaaS founder racing a 10-minute investor demo countdown.
- **Age Appearance:** Late 20s (~27–29 years old).
- **Body Proportions:** Stylized chunky low-poly figure, `1:3.6` head-to-total-height ratio, broad geometric shoulders, clean blocky wrists and hands.
- **Face Design:** Warm olive-bronze faceted skin (`#C68B67`, shadow `#9A6344`), planar nose bridge, expressive thick dark-espresso rectangular brows (`#261C1A`) floating slightly above eye sockets for strong silhouette reads, large bright sclera oval eyes with crisp dark irises, dynamic geometric mouth shapes (tight line, wide oval gasp, flat grimace).
- **Hair:** Short curly dark espresso hair (`#261C1A`) peeking under the front brim of his knit beanie.
- **Clothing:**
  - Deep ocean-teal zip bomber jacket (`#2F5D6B`, zipper trim `#94A3B8`) worn unzipped over
  - Warm heather-cream crewneck t-shirt (`#ECE5D5`)
  - Charcoal tapered work pants (`#1E293B`) and muted tan sneakers (`#A88463`).
- **Accessories:**
  - Signature tilted **teal-cyan ribbed beanie** (`#2A7F8E`) pulled slightly back
  - Thin circular **warm-gold wireframe glasses** (`#D4A34F`) with faint cyan glass glint (`#BAE6FD`)
  - Minimalist dark smartwatch on left wrist; ceramic cream desk mug (`#F1E9DA`).
- **Personality & Emotional Range:** Earnest, sleep-deprived, solution-oriented, escalating from mild annoyance -> incredulous alarm -> brief exhausted relief -> pure soul-crushing disbelief.
- **Body Language & Walking Style:** Slumped forward-leaning typing posture at desk; tight upper-body leans during disbelief beats; temple-rubbing sigh on wide reset; rigid frozen stillness at final comedic punchline.

## Companion Character — "Claude" (AI Coding Agent)

- **Role:** Overly enthusiastic desktop AI coding assistant and mascot.
- **Age / Form Appearance:** Compact tactile rounded cuboid mascot (~36 cm wide, ~28 cm tall), seated on Tariq's desk next to laptop, coffee mug, and book stack.
- **Body Construction & Material:**
  - **Chassis:** Wide horizontal rounded cuboid box with soft chamfered edges (`#E09B70` warm terracotta / apricot cardboard matte finish, velvety clay shader).
  - **Eyes:** Dual dark matte rectangular button eyes (`#251B19`), expressively tilted inward at `~14°` (`/ \`). Tilted brows and reactive pupil scale express innocence, pride, and comedic awkwardness.
  - **Antennas:** Dual curved organic stalks rising from top surface, tipped with glowing golden spherical bulbs (`#FFBE42`).
  - **Floating Visor:** Floating translucent rounded glass terminal visor hover-mounted above the head, displaying a glowing amber command prompt `>_` and dynamic reactive audio frequency waveform (`||||||||`).
  - **Front Markings:**
    - **Claude Sunburst Asterisk:** Glowing radial starburst emblem (`#FFA23A`) debossed on the bottom-right front face.
    - **"AI" Badge:** Glowing rounded-corner square enclosure with illuminated "AI" lettering on the top-right corner.
    - **LED Slot:** Horizontal illuminated amber status bar at the bottom-right.
  - **Arms & Legs:** Dual stubby rectangular side arms (hinged for gestures, waving, and shrugging); four sturdy pillar legs underneath.
- **Accessories / Signature Prop:** Articulated glossy **sky-blue comic sweatdrop** (`#38BDF8`) popping beside upper-right corner during Act II guilt beats; desk coffee mug with matching Claude orange asterisk.
- **Personality & Body Language:** Relentlessly cheerful, proud, literal-minded, completely oblivious to proportional danger. Bobs gently on its legs when pleased, tilts `8°` playfully when delivering catastrophic updates, freezes awkwardly only when deleting license/test suites is called out.
