## Scene 01: The Phone Hook (00:00.0 – 00:04.0 / frames 0–120)
- **Framing:** Low-angle 3D Medium Shot (`phone_hero`)
- **Visuals:** Claude stands atop an ultra-thin glass smartphone laying flat on a dark wooden studio table. Warm amber desk lamp casts a cozy rim light. Claude's antenna tips glow with warm golden light. The floating HUD visor above its eyes displays glowing terminal prompt `>_` and dynamic audio spectrum bars that bounce as it speaks.
- **Camera Movement:** Slow cinematic push-in toward Claude (`z -= 0.12`).
- **Dialogue (Claude):** *"You don't have to stay glued to your desk while I rebuild your frontend. I can now text your phone."*
- **Graphic Overlay:** Glowing notification bubble pops on the phone screen below Claude's feet: `💬 Claude: "Finished authentication refactor!"`
- **Transition:** Quick whip-pan to Scene 02.

## Scene 02: The Frozen Terminal Dilemma (00:04.0 – 00:08.2 / frames 120–246)
- **Framing:** Medium Two-Shot with Holographic Screen (`terminal_dilemma`)
- **Visuals:** Claude stands next to a floating translucent terminal window in midnight navy. The terminal shows:
  ```bash
  $ claude code
  > Building bundle... (14/20 files)
  ? Do you want to execute migrations on production? (y/N) █
  ```
  A flashing red clock icon indicates the process is frozen waiting for human approval while the developer is away.
- **Dialogue (Claude):** *"Leaving your laptop used to mean paused terminal prompts and stalled workflows. Not anymore."*
- **Animation:** Claude raises its stubby arm, shaking its head with tilted button eyes showing mild frustration.
- **Transition:** Holographic terminal card dissolves into radiant particle burst.
