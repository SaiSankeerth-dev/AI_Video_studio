## 2. Personality & Presenter Voice

- **Persona:** Knowledgeable, friendly, slightly mischievous, immensely proud of its capabilities, but genuinely caring about developer comfort.
- **Presenter Energy:** Enthusiastic, conversational, fast-paced short-form delivery with playful comedic timing.
- **Vocal Attributes:** Synthesized via Kokoro-82M (`af_nova`), pitched slightly bright (+1.05) with crisp articulation and natural conversational pauses.

## 3. Brand Environment (Agent Talk Visual Identity)

- **Studio Palette:**
  - Ambient base: Deep midnight navy (`#0B111E`) and moody indigo purple (`#17122E`).
  - Warm Key / Highlights: Radiant tungsten orange (`#FF9500` / `#FFAE42`) from desk lamps and neon tubes.
  - Accent Neons: Cyber emerald (`#10B981`) for code success/merges, electric cyan (`#38BDF8`) for mobile app screens.
- **Lighting Setup:**
  - Key Light: Warm softbox at 45° angle.
  - Rim Light: Golden glow from antenna tips and background fairy lights.
  - Fill Light: Soft cool blue bounce preventing pitch-black shadows.
