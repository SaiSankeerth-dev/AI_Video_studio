## 3. Visual Pacing & Stagnation Defense
1. **The 2.5-Second Threshold**: No static frame persists longer than 2.8 seconds without either:
   - A camera scale change (push-in / pull-out)
   - A kinetic typography highlight
   - A pan / directional drift
   - An optical scanline or graphic overlay
2. **Dynamic Range in Editing**: Rapid fast-paced cuts during the investigative montage (Shots 04–07) contrast sharply with deliberate, breath-holding slow pushes during the cutting of the box (Shots 14–16).
3. **No Dead Air / Zero Fluff**: Every sentence carries direct evidence or narrative advancement.
