A company’s security team had done everything right. Hardened firewalls, complex passwords, and round-the-clock intrusion detection. And yet, an intruder still walked away with their most sensitive proprietary source code.

The attacker didn't write an exploit to breach the system. He simply picked up a telephone.

This is the definitive story of Kevin Mitnick—and the idea that transformed modern cybersecurity forever: Social Engineering.

From compromising Motorola and Digital Equipment Corporation to the nationwide FBI pursuit and his eventual transformation into a trusted security educator, discover why the weakest link in any digital fortress isn't software... it's human trust.
