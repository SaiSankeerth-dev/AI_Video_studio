For years, artificial intelligence waited for us to ask a question. But over the past 12 months, the paradigm of computing underwent a quiet, fundamental fracture: we stopped building models that just talk, and started building systems that act.

In this documentary, we deconstruct the shift from passive chatbots to autonomous agentic systems—how they use tools, execute terminal commands, edit code, self-heal, and coordinate in multi-agent enterprise swarms.
